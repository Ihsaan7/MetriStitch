Good input saves a revision round. This is what helps most.

## Send the best version you have

Vector files and PDFs are ideal. A clear, large image also works. If you only have a photo of a design, send it anyway and say so.

## Tell us three things

1. **Size.** How wide the design will be when stitched.
2. **Placement.** Left chest, cap front, sleeve, back and so on.
3. **Fabric.** For example polo cloth, twill, canvas or a vestment fabric.

These decide how a design is digitized, so they are worth a line in the form.

## Tell us the format

Say which format your machine reads. We deliver {{fact:formatsAnd}}, and other formats on request.
